About Me

Hello, I’m Shraddha Sri—a passionate writer, lifelong dreamer, and a student on the path to becoming a doctor. While my days are filled with the rhythm of NEET preparation, my heart finds peace in poetry and prose.

Writing is my sanctuary. My pen name, Dabria Hancy, holds a quiet power—it’s the voice I lend to my verses, stories, and silent thoughts. Through metaphors and moonlight, I try to weave emotions that people carry but rarely speak.

I write because words stay when everything else shifts. Welcome to my quiet corner of the universe.

The Endless Journey: Story of a Waterfall

From the peak of the hill, I stand,
My journey begins, wild and grand,
Down rocky cliffs, through forests deep,
Where secrets of the ancients sleep.

I leap with joy from edge to edge,
Carving stories through every ledge.
In silver threads I softly stream,
A poet’s pulse, a painter’s dream.

I sing to birds, I race with breeze,
I kiss the roots of ancient trees.
The sun reflects my crystal face,
In endless fall, I find my grace.

I carry tales of mountain skies,
Of howls and hushes, lows and highs.
Each drop I shed, a tale set free—
A verse of water’s legacy.

And though I crash, and roar, and sway,
I never once have lost my way.
For in the fall, I rise anew,
With strength the skies and stars once knew.

— Dabria Hancy

The Unexpected Meeting

It was a typical rainy afternoon in the city when Riya found herself at the small, cozy café near her office. As she stirred her drink, she couldn’t shake the feeling that something wasn’t quite right. Her phone had been buzzing non-stop all day...

Then the door jingled. In walked Aryan—her childhood friend. He looked different—confident, polished—but his eyes still held that spark. They chatted, laughed, as if no time had passed. But just as things felt normal again, Aryan’s phone buzzed. He looked at the screen, and his smile faded. "I… I have to go," he said. And just like that, he was gone. But Riya knew: this wasn’t the end. Something was coming. And it had everything to do with her.
